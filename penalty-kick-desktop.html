HOW TO PLAY

1. Striker Turn: Click and drag the ball towards the goal. The faster you swipe, the more power and curve!

2. Goalkeeper Turn: Move your mouse or finger across the goal frame to position the green gloves and make the save.

⚽ PENALTY KICK ONLINE — 3D WORLD SHOOTOUT
TOURNAMENT RULES FIFA RULES

• Best of 5 Shootout: Each team takes 5 kicks alternating between striker and goalkeeper.

• Sudden Death: If tied after 5 rounds, kicks continue one-by-one until a winner emerges.

• Ball Physics: Curving shots to top corners gives highest scoring chance against CPU keeper.
